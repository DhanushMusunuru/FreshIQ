FreshIQ wiring

Before first power-up

VDD must be 3.3 V

The A4988 reads logic HIGH at roughly 0.7 × VDD. On 5 V that threshold is 3.5 V and the UNO Q's outputs only reach 3.3 V, so STEP is never reliably seen. The motor then twitches, or works until it warms up — a fault that reads like a dead driver.

JP1 is not a factory jumper

RESET and SLEEP both float, and a floating RESET holds the chip in reset forever. Nothing moves and nothing is damaged, which is why people replace working drivers over it.

Fit C1 first

100 µF across VMOT and GND, within 50 mm of the driver. Motor inductance plus supply leads makes an LC circuit; powering up without bulk capacitance produces a spike well past 12 V that destroys the A4988. Same failure if you unplug the motor while powered.

Current limit

Set it with a multimeter between the trimpot wiper and ground before the first move: Vref = I × 8 × Rsense. For 1.0 A with R100 (0.1 Ω) sense resistors that is 0.80 V; with R050 it is 0.40 V. The 17HS4401 is rated 1.68 A, but this tray is light and slow — 1.0 A runs cooler and costs nothing here.
